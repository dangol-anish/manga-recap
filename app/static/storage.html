Protect your work

Create a full backup

Includes original manga, narration, saved progress, panel fixes, audio and videos. API keys, installed packages, models, caches and runtime logs are excluded. Files stay unchanged.

Backups are verified ZIP files in this project’s backups folder. Copy them to another drive for protection against drive failure. They are kept out of Git.

Bring saved work back

Restore a backup

To bring a backup from another computer, copy its ZIP into the backups folder above. Reinstall the app and models separately, and add your API keys to .env.

Off by default. Identical files are reused. Differing files block restore unless you explicitly allow replacement. Replacement returns narration and saved progress to their backup versions; your current versions are kept separately. Files absent from the backup are kept. Avoid editing files in other programs during restore.
