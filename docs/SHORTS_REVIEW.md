# Reviewing a short before creating it

Open **Shorts**, choose an existing project (or import suggestions normally), then press **Review story & pacing** on an idea. This reads saved files only: no Gemini call, new voice, video creation or automatic script edits.

- Read the **Opening** and **Ending** together. Decide whether the opening gives a reason to stay and the ending delivers a supported payoff. The app does not score viral potential.
- Choose a segment to see its panel, short script, original source beat and any source uncertainty. A source beat can cover several panels. The original narration may itself have AI errors, so this comparison is assistance, not factual verification. The existing optional Gemini visual check remains available.
- Inspect the caption chunks. They use the video's existing grouping rule. This text view does not simulate exact font placement, wrapping, camera motion or platform overlays.
- Before audio exists, durations are estimates based on the project's saved speed. If some matching clips exist, those durations are measured and the others estimated; the overall timeline is still provisional. Once all clips match, the timeline and caption cue times use their saved timestamps.
- If a current finished MP4 and all matching clips exist, **Jump to this panel in saved video** pauses that actual video at the panel's first frame. Press its native Play button to listen.
- Suggestions identify very brief or long holds, consecutive reuse of the same panel, original uncertainty and a likely/measured duration outside 30–120 seconds. They do not block creation or change the script. The existing final duration validation still runs after audio generation.

Editing the script immediately pauses/hides its old video and removes the old review. **Discard unsaved edits** restores the saved script locally without writing or generating anything. **Save script** is available only for changed drafts. Save or discard before reviewing. Existing projects retain their saved voice and speed, now displayed correctly on the page.

Reviews and display updates do not rewrite projects, recordings, captions or MP4s. Imported source text, panel hashes and prior-story lineage remain freshness-checked; a change to context display wording or review overlays alone does not invalidate an unchanged existing project. The original immutable package receipt and clip tokens are retained.
