# Reviewing panel switches

Open **Stage 7 → Video**, then choose a chapter with current audio. Nothing is created just by opening the page.

- If a finished current video exists, use its native Play, Seek and Fullscreen controls. Otherwise press **Create video** first.
- **Check panel pacing** lists panel holds shorter than 0.7 seconds or longer than 15 seconds, measured at the actual video frame boundaries. These are suggestions, not mistakes: brief reactions and long narrated panels may be intentional. Select a suggestion to open its beat. The first 50 are listed to keep the page light; all panels remain accessible in the beat selector.
- Under **Panel timing**, **Jump to panel** pauses the finished MP4 at that panel's first frame. **Watch switch** plays up to 1.25 seconds before and after the switch, within its beat, then stops. For the first panel it starts at the beat's opening. Seek away or pause to end a review.
- Adjust a later panel's switch using **−1 frame / +1 frame** (1/24 second), or type a time in seconds relative to the beat. **Use video position** takes the native player's current position and converts it to beat-relative seconds. A panel must retain at least one frame.
- **Discard unsaved changes** restores the selected beat's saved timing, without writing anything. **Reset to phrase timing** stages the original phrase switches as an unsaved draft; it does not silently save them.
- Press **Save timing** only when ready. Then press **Create video** to see the updated result. The previous MP4 stays on disk; it is not presented as matching the new timing. Jump/watch actions are unavailable while timing is unsaved or the current MP4 has not been created.

These controls never synthesize speech, rewrite narration, run detection, alter crops or change the video style. Scripts, audio, existing videos and earlier timing revisions stay safe. The video format, blurred background, motion and brief transitions are unchanged.
